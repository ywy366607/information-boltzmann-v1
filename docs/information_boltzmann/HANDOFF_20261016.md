   the write drive by 56% but still writes 39% of edges per word, with surprisal ρ 0.07. My prediction (< 10%) failed:
   integrated passes come in runs.

   MBON population response over repeats is about 0, so φ does not reach the MBONs. Earlier finding: the Kenyon share of
   MBON drive is small.

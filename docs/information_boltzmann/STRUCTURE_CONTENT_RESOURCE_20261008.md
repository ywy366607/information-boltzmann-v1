# Joint structure/content/resource numerical candidate

## Goal and scope

The target remains real-data prediction and active recovery on a continuous
individual. Current D768 8x8x8 OWT training remains the baseline; this CPU-only
prototype introduces no capability task and performs zero optimizer updates.

Three separate questions are tested: whether structural changes preserve stored
state, whether they change finite-time observable distinctions, and whether
finite resources create allocation competition. These are independent of whether
the resulting structure improves language or old semantic retrieval.

## Candidate and sigma-algebra interpretation

Use the existing material and persistent state coordinates. Structure m changes
the finite-time observation map R_m. The sigma-algebra generated by R_m describes
distinctions available at that port and horizon; the stored state supplies content.
An invertible recoding of observations generates the same sigma-algebra, so every
weight change must not be described as a newly learned distinction. A changed
kernel in the linear conservative numerical test gives a concrete counterexample:
a state difference invisible before becomes visible after a structural change.
This is not a finite implementation of a complete learned sigma-algebra.

## Single structural constraint

Define C(B)=mean_x trace(BB^T), a propagation-capacity proxy invariant under
orthogonal changes of flux basis. Set the candidate budget from initial capacity,
and apply B_eff=B min(1,sqrt(C_birth/C(B))) separately for each individual.
The integral uses a quadrature mean, so changing grid resolution does not create
extra resources. Above the budget the derivative couples sites, making increased
allocation to one site compete with the rest. The radial derivative is zero.
No dual penalty is added: projection already enforces the constraint.

This is a fixed capacity ceiling, not a measured biological metabolic law.
Maintenance is a rate, growth has a change cost, and resource supply can vary;
those are not implemented here. A proof/test also shows that this quadratic
budget alone permits both a uniform and a concentrated strictly SPD material
with identical cost. It therefore cannot certify clear channels or branching.
Task gradients must supply differential value; a different cost law needs its
own constitutive justification and cannot be selected merely for attractive images.

## Numerical predictions and findings

- Projection preserves SPD and relative anisotropy for positive budget and
  finite invertible input; gradients agree with finite differences away from
  the switching boundary.
- Changing only structure leaves all existing MediumState coordinates and
  elapsed time unchanged; subsequent projected-factor transport preserves
  activity energy and field integral.
- A prescribed constitutive change at fixed capacity changes a finite-time
  local observation kernel. It may also alter the interpretation of old state;
  exact stored values do not guarantee semantic readout stability.
- Budget alone has no preference between equal-cost spatial allocations.

Results are in results/published/medium_structural_resource_20261008.json.
Independent review approved the scope as a numerical prototype and identified
the distinction between capacity, metabolism, and semantic retention. Production
integration is now available as an optional constitutive setting. Performance
hypotheses remain open for a matched real-data comparison.

## Decision

Keep this as an opt-in constitutive candidate; the running individual is unchanged.
The single projection is wired into transport and read-RHS coefficient paths,
with checkpoint budget validation. Use `--transport-capacity-budget birth`
to take the ceiling from the initialized slow material, or pass a positive
explicit capacity. No extra neural network or learned parameters are added.

Competition applies to slow structural capacity, before local activity/STP
modulation. Instantaneous effective capacity may therefore differ from the
ceiling. This preserves local fast dynamics: an activity change at one site
does not instantaneously rescale propagation everywhere. Numerical tests cover
this locality contract and the common transport/read-RHS coefficients.

Compare matched active OWT streams
with at least the authorized 5000 updates. Reject a benefit claim if only the
material image changes or old-information recovery worsens without compensating
predictive benefit. No synthetic capability training is needed as a prerequisite.

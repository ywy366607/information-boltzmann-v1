# MengLi entrance: source and modifications

The entrance's staged waiting, timer/waiter cancellation, skip/replay cleanup,
and dark-curtain/bloom/handoff transition patterns are adapted from
**dsh-startup-screen**, package version **1.3.0**, by 沈宏宏:

- https://github.com/6shenhonghong9/dsh-startup-screen
- https://github.com/6shenhonghong9/dsh-startup-screen/blob/main/lib/splash.js
- https://github.com/6shenhonghong9/dsh-startup-screen/blob/main/lib/splash.css
- https://github.com/6shenhonghong9/dsh-startup-screen/blob/main/LICENSE

Adaptation: standalone six-second MengLi visual entrance, original membrane/seed
artwork, new copy and palette, reduced-motion handling, keyboard dismissal,
per-tab replay preference, and isolated cleanup. It has no model/session controls.
The upstream product integration, access-verification sequence, audio recordings,
sound assets, logos, and external fonts are not included. The MengLi mark is new
artwork, not the Rhine Lab emblem. This is not an official Rhine Lab or Arknights
product.
